SOCIETY FIELD GUIDE

Bring a bigger cart.

Dr. Pip is searching ancient ruins for history. Baby Bean is searching for everything Pip just dropped.

  1. Walk the desert. Hold the arrow buttons, use ← → or A / D, or click the sand to choose a destination. Double-click anywhere in the scene to run to the ground beneath it. Hold Shift or the Run button to move faster.
  2. Poke holes. Look for small sand mounds, cracks, and scattered stones. Click a suspicious patch to walk over and jab. Finds and wall alcoves take one to four pecks. In the jungle, the first peck breaks roots covering tangled treasures. Space or P pokes just ahead of your beak. Some finds leave no surface clue at all. Press S or Sniff to reveal nearby hidden spots for six seconds. Once poked, a hole remains visible.
  3. Leave it to Bean. Your tiny assistant picks up discoveries, carries them back, and tosses them onto the cart. A cache of chickens means multiple chickens.
  4. Question your packing. Bean loads heavy finds low. Pause for a few seconds after pickups and he climbs aboard to restack and tie a separate rope around each item. Each finished knot stays tied if you move away. New loose finds can still fall until he repacks. Bean tries to catch reachable pieces and retrieves anything he misses. A tall loose pile can topple even while parked. Bean applies the brakes, reloads and ties each item individually, then releases Pip once the cart is secure.
  5. Enter a pyramid. Click a doorway or use Pyramid ↓ / E. Pip can leave while Bean is still collecting. Your finds travel between rooms, and Bean resumes work on the next floor. Leaving the whole site deposits the collection. Directional signs must be clicked; E takes the nearest ordinary doorway.
  6. Reach the oasis. Explore the desert, the oasis, the pyramid chambers, and the Great Tomb deeper inside. Each area succeeds once Bean has collected and individually tied down every item. Earlier collections live in the expandable warehouse manifest. Egyptian antiquities and complete nonsense receive equal academic respect.
  7. Follow the expedition. Finish the four Egyptian areas to unlock Naxos in the oasis. Finish the four Greek areas to reveal the Jungle → sign in the Olive Grove. Explore the Canopy Trail, Rain Courtyard, Root Vault and Heart of the Temple. Finish the jungle to unlock Baths → on the Canopy Trail. Explore the Aqueduct Garden, Mosaic Baths, and Under the Baths; then follow Castle → from the garden to the Cliffside Castle, Clockwork Hall, and Stargazer’s Tower. The cart stays loaded throughout each expedition. Express lifts in the final room carry Pip, Bean and the collection straight back to the outdoor scene; click the wooden platform or use its Lift button.

Progress saves automatically. Click a manifest item for its field notes. Escape closes these pages.
