# New button upgrade artwork

Seven transparent assembled PNGs, generated with built-in ImageGen using the approved kiwi and button as references. Union is dropped. Salad bar supersedes the earlier buffet table concept; button activities replace the inspector concept. Prices and integration are left to Clicker.

The assembled PNGs are static references. The revised deterministic moving layers, timing, paths and pivots are supplied in [animation-v2/manifest.json](animation-v2/manifest.json), with a [230px animated preview](animation-v2/preview.html) and integration notes in [animation-v2/README.md](animation-v2/README.md). Earlier generated four-frame pose sheets are superseded.

- [Worm salad bar](salad-bar/assembled-idle.png): Static no-kiwi picture; no animation.
- [Button slide](slide/assembled-idle.png): Move kiwi down curved slide; depress each cap as it passes.
- [Button trampoline](trampoline/assembled-idle.png): Bounce kiwi vertically; cap compresses at landing.
- [Button seesaw](seesaw/assembled-idle.png): Rock board around central pivot; alternate foot presses.
- [Button staircase](staircase/assembled-idle.png): Hop down one step at a time; depress occupied cap.
- [Pecking carousel](carousel/assembled-idle.png): Rotate button platform; peck when a button reaches the bill.
- [Kiwi conga line](conga/assembled-idle.png): Staggered dance in place with a lead-bird press.
