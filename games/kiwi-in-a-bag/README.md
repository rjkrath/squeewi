# Kiwi in a Bag

An independent, dependency-free canvas game at `/play/kiwi-in-a-bag/`.

Tap a bag or its matching button: the kiwi walks over and plays automatically. Tap the floor to move, including waddling inside the empty bag. Tap the occupied bag or use Hop out to leave it. An accidentally worn bag comes off with one tap on the kiwi or Shake it off. The sideways bag starts an automatic run-up, dive, and slowing slide across the rug. It slides back into place during the next run-up for repeat play. The mystery bag reveals a tiny bag holding the sock, crumbs, and worm, with a repeatable rummaging animation. Arrows/WASD move and Space/E interact for keyboard play.

Nine bag types are spread across three connected rooms: the central sitting room has hop-in, slide, and rummage bags; the left quiet corner has tiny, peekaboo, and nap bags; the right play room has tunnel, pounce, and shared bags. Tap a left/right caret to cut immediately to the adjacent room, followed by a brief kiwi entrance from the matching edge. Keyboard walking to an edge also crosses rooms. Outer rooms have a reciprocal caret back to the center. Tap any bag to play its animation in place. The last room persists on reload; the kiwi restores safely. Optional keyboard-accessible bag buttons and discoveries are in expandable panels beneath the scene. Six tap-to-play antics join the original three bags in that grid: approaching, hopping, and visibly wriggling into a too-small bag with the head and shoulders overflowing its rim, peekaboo after walking over and hopping through the bag opening, a three-bag tunnel, a crinkle pounce that flattens a bag, turning around for a bag nap, and two kiwis sharing and tipping one bag: the first walks over and sniffs alone, then the second arrives from off-screen and hops in; the first follows. Left-room bags keep the same size when idle and active. Each has a timed animation and a resting pose, plus Again and Back to the bags. Switching antics or returning early safely cancels the current animation.

Discoveries, tried antics, and completed bags persist under `squeewi:kiwi-in-a-bag:v1`. Existing spill and paper-maze saves retain discoveries and restart in the room. Reload always places the kiwi safely in the room. Malformed saves are sanitized; storage failures remain playable. Audio starts muted on every visit and uses locally synthesized crinkles. No external assets or services. Reduced motion removes wobbling and hopping animations. Reset requires a native dialog confirmation.

Validation: `node --test tests/kiwi-in-a-bag.test.mjs`, `npm run build`, and browser checks.

Animation consistency checks sample the actual renderer across all nine actions. They verify idle-to-active bag sizes and floor anchors, room-scale kiwi drawing, finite coordinates, and bag positions within the room. Approaches use the visible kiwi before entering an animation; the nap visibly hops in and the shared bag bulges gradually.
